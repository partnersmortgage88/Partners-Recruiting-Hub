How we work · Loan Officer

Loan Officer responsibilities.

Essential duties & workflow overview — you stay on relationships and strategy; your LOA and processor carry the file.

Lead engagement & intake
  • Make initial contact with lead and send the ‘Apply Now’ link
  • Follow up regularly until the application is submitted
  • Notify LOA once the application is in; provide borrower / preapproval context
Application & preapproval
  • Pull credit report (if LOA is unlicensed)
  • Once LOA confirms borrower is ready and provides base numbers: conduct a strategic consultation to explore loan options and scenarios
  • Issue the official Pre-Approval Letter once structure is decided
  • Notify LOA of final structure; update ARIVE and CRM notes
Preapproval to contract
  • Maintain borrower communication from preapproval through contract acceptance (purchase) or decision to proceed (refi / HELOC)
  • Once in contract, email LOA: final loan structure (amount, rate, investor), fully executed purchase contract (if applicable), escrow / title information
Locking & disclosures
  • Once LOA uploads to the investor portal: lock the loan (or give a licensed LOA lock guidance if floating)
  • Confirm all pricing details including discount / origination points for disclosures
Pipeline communication & problem-solving
  • Communicate status updates to borrower and agents throughout the loan
  • Notify borrower when contingencies are met
  • If a loan is suspended or has underwriting, appraisal, or condition issues: LO communicates directly with the borrower and collaborates with LOA and processor on resolution strategies
Closing coordination
  • Follow up with the processor consistently from underwriting through funding
  • Coordinate signing with borrower and escrow
  • Ensure a smooth, timely close and a positive borrower experience
How we work · Loan Officer Assistant

Loan Officer Assistant responsibilities.

Essential duties & workflow overview — the engine room behind every file.

File setup & application review
  • Review new applications; structure the ARIVE file per LO notes
  • Pull credit report (if licensed)
  • Review, approve, rename and organize all documents uploaded to ARIVE or emailed
Borrower communication & docs follow-up
  • Prepare and send the needs-list email to borrower, CC the Loan Officer
  • Follow up until all documents and information are received
Income & preapproval prep
  • Send employment verifications; accurately calculate qualifying income (income worksheet provided)
  • Once docs and income are complete, prepare the base preapproval summary and send to LO
Contract intake & escrow coordination
  • Once in contract or ready for refi / HELOC: receive the fully executed contract and escrow details
  • Send the escrow request and save documents to ARIVE
File structuring & property research
  • Comprehensively review the contract; complete ARIVE file setup
  • Pull property details and tax bill; calculate property taxes (tax worksheet provided)
  • Research HOA or solar through Zillow, Redfin, etc.
Lock preparation & investor upload
  • Prepare an updated needs list; update borrower on next steps
  • Confirm investor with LO; upload the .XML FNMA file to the investor portal
  • Confirm lock guidance and discount / origination point details
Loan submission & processing
  • Prepare the Underwriter Cover Letter summarizing the loan (template provided)
  • Complete the ‘Loan Setup’ tab in ARIVE; send the submission email to the processor
  • Attach title fee sheet, lock (if applicable) and cover letter
Disclosures & underwriting prep
  • Follow up with borrower until disclosures are signed
  • Once signed, review documentation and confirm readiness
  • Notify processor with final structure, document status, and underwriting readiness
